About

A bit about me

I'm a Data Science graduate student at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I'm drawn to problems at the intersection of deep learning, recommender systems, and responsible AI.

Before starting my Master's, I spent two and a half years at Kyndryl as a Technical Engineer, managing ETL batch workflows across four enterprise clients at 99.9% pipeline reliability. That experience taught me to think carefully about data integrity, documentation, and ownership of unfamiliar problems.

During my Bachelor's at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, I built and published a content-based recommendation system at NCECA 2023 using dual neural networks with a custom cost function and L2 regularisation. I'm currently exploring machine unlearning — how models can forget data verifiably, and how unlearning interacts with fairness.

Research

Published work

NCECA 2023 · Published

Enhanced Customer Content-Based Recommendation System

Proceedings of the National Conference on Emerging Computer Applications (NCECA-2023)  ·  DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.7965018

A deep learning approach to content-based filtering: dual neural networks (user network + movie network) learn 32-dimensional embeddings, combined via dot product to predict ratings. Trained with a custom cost function, L2 regularisation, and the Adam optimiser; evaluated on AUC, log loss, and recall against collaborative, hybrid, classification-based, and knowledge-based baselines; deployed logic follows a retrieval-and-ranking pipeline for efficiency on large catalogues.
