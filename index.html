About

My first real piece of work was a content-based recommendation system — a dual-network architecture, published and presented at NCECA 2023. It was the project that convinced me machine learning was where I wanted to end up.

I took the long way there. At Kyndryl I started as a mainframe z/OS operator on shift rotation, running batch workflows for four enterprise clients — Honda, TATA Steel, ASR and Ethniki Insurance — systems those businesses genuinely cannot operate without. Later I moved to the Agentic AI team, building real-time dashboards on Kyndryl Bridge. Two and a half years of that teaches you what "critical" actually means when something breaks at 3 a.m.

In April 2026 I moved to Germany to begin an M.Sc. in Data Science at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, specialising in AI and machine learning. Alongside it I'm shaping a side project of my own, still early.

Now I'm looking for a Werkstudent role in AI or data — bringing production experience most students don't have, and every intention of learning fast inside a good team.
