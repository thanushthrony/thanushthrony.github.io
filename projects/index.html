Enhanced Content-Based Movie Recommender

Published — NCECA 2023 Deep Learning

My Bachelor's thesis, published at NCECA 2023: a dual neural network learns 32-dimensional embeddings for users and movies from content features, predicting ratings with a single dot product. The demo runs the actual trained model — weights extracted from the original Keras network — entirely in your browser, on the 847-movie MovieLens catalogue it was trained on. Pick movies you like and watch profile-building, retrieval, and ranking happen live.
